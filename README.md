# 🥋 The Data Science Dojo

> *"Wax on, wax off. First learn stand, then learn fly. Nature rule, Daniel-san, not mine."*

Welcome to your journey. This repository is your **dojo** — the place where you
train, fall, get up, and train again. There are no shortcuts here. Only
discipline, repetition, and curiosity.

You are the student. I am your mentor. We go one step at a time.

---

## 📜 The Way of the Data Scientist

Data Science stands on four legs, like a strong table. Remove one, and the table
falls. We will train each leg until it is strong.

| Leg | What it is | Why it matters |
|-----|-----------|----------------|
| 🐍 **Programming** | Python, the language of the craft | Your hands. Without them you cannot move. |
| 📊 **Statistics & Math** | Probability, linear algebra, calculus | Your eyes. They let you *see* the truth in data. |
| 🧹 **Data Wrangling** | Cleaning, shaping, exploring data | Real data is dirty. 80% of the work lives here. |
| 🤖 **Machine Learning** | Models that learn from data | The flying. But only *after* you can stand. |

---

## 🗺️ The Path (Your Belts)

We earn belts by doing, not by reading.

- [ ] **⚪ White Belt** — Python fundamentals (variables, loops, functions)
- [ ] **🟡 Yellow Belt** — NumPy & Pandas (the data scientist's hands)
- [ ] **🟢 Green Belt** — Data visualization & exploratory analysis (EDA)
- [ ] **🔵 Blue Belt** — Statistics & probability foundations
- [ ] **🟤 Brown Belt** — Machine learning fundamentals (regression, classification)
- [ ] **⚫ Black Belt** — A full project, end to end, by your own hands

## 🧘 The Rules of the Dojo

1. **Type every line yourself.** Do not copy-paste. The hand must remember.
2. **Break things on purpose.** Understanding comes from fixing what you broke.
3. **Ask "why" three times.** Why does this work? Why this way? Why not another?
4. **Commit small and often.** Each commit is a step. Many steps make a journey.
5. **Patience.** You do not become a black belt in a weekend.
